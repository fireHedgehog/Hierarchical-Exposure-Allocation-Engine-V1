## Hypothesis

Gold may increasingly express fiscal-dominance, sovereign-credibility, or currency-
debasement concerns rather than only the textbook real-yield mechanism. Repeated
gold strength alongside rising yields or a firm dollar, when contemporaneous
reporting attributes the move to fiscal concerns, would support that structural
shift.

The hypothesis is weakened if later observations consistently return to the
textbook relationship and the apparent exceptions do not repeat.

## Observation contract

For each event, record gold direction, real-yield direction, USD direction, Fed
stance, the fiscal or sovereign event, and the external source's attribution.
Leave unavailable fields null. Do not infer causality from price co-movement.

## Honest conclusion

One event is not a gold reaction function. It is a dated reason to continue
observing rather than hard-code GLD as a purely Fed-dovish or real-yield asset.
