# Capability sequence from demonstration to real decisions

[Edition V2](editions/edition-v2.md) preserves the durable design; this page defines the implementation sequence and the evidence required before a surface can represent real rather than synthetic decisions. Operations → Pipeline is the live, database-backed readiness view. Each phase should ship as a narrow, visible vertical slice; later gaps do not prevent useful earlier work. Completion means persisted, inspectable behavior in the application, not a persuasive document or one successful backtest.

| Phase | Current gap | Completion evidence | UI surface unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Macro source to state | FRED access can be verified, but no production adapter, point-in-time observations, sealed dataset, or computed regime exists. | Repeatable paced ingestion with ALFRED vintage metadata; timestamp, unit, lineage, freshness, coverage, and revision validation; immutable dataset hash; deterministic manual regime run. | Data Management, Runs, Today, and the regime branch of the hierarchy show real classified inputs and blockers. |
| 2. Security master and universes | The 21-symbol staging universe (`staging_symbols`) is a free-data fixture, database-driven but flat — no ticker history, delistings, corporate actions, or effective-dated membership revisions. | Stable exposure, research-reference, security, and provider identities; ticker history, listings, delistings, corporate actions, effective-dated universe membership and reasons; an immutable universe revision tied to a sealed market dataset. | Data Management and Symbols can distinguish underlying research references, the full eligible universe, and the currently available candidate set. |
| 3. Broad risk and sleeve allocation | `allocation_engine` is real: regime confidence sets a gross-exposure multiplier and cross-sectional composites roll up into sleeve targets. No sensitivity testing, no covariance-aware sizing, no policy revisioning — one naive formula, not yet optimized (Milestone 4). | A revisioned policy maps the real state and uncertainty to portfolio risk limits and broad asset or strategy sleeve budgets, with sensitivity tests and explicit null behavior. | Today and Hierarchy explain why capital is or is not available before individual names are considered. |
| 4. Cross-sectional discovery | `factor_engine` is real: a naive blended 1M/3M/6M z-score momentum ranker over the staging universe. No declared hypothesis/baseline framework, no IC, decay, turnover, cost, capacity, stability, or walk-forward evidence tracked yet. | One revisioned selector with a declared hypothesis and baseline produces point-in-time ranks within peer groups and records coverage, IC, decay, turnover, costs, capacity, stability, and walk-forward evidence. | Cross-section becomes a dynamic discovery surface with ranked candidates and attributable factor contributions. |
| 5. Single-name timing | A real naive MACD/RSI backtest computes real per-symbol entry/exit signals and a full trade log, plus a desk-level aggregate — it currently loses to buy-and-hold on average. No leakage, cost, decay, or out-of-sample validation framework, and it is not yet a separately revisioned stage from cross-sectional discovery (both live in `factor_engine`). | A separately revisioned time-series model consumes eligible candidates, emits `enter`, `wait`, `hold`, `exit`, or `none`, and passes leakage, timing, cost, decay, and out-of-sample checks. | Symbol pages explain current timing, chart events, historical decisions, metrics, decay, and failure states. |
| 6. Portfolio construction | `allocation_engine`/`instrument_engine` produce real sleeve targets and per-symbol candidate deltas, but there is no optimizer reconciling covariance, overlap, sector, concentration, or liquidity constraints. | Reproducible target weights and deltas satisfy net/gross, covariance, overlap, factor, sector, concentration, liquidity, turnover, and scenario constraints, including honest infeasibility. | Today and Hierarchy show the proposed portfolio, binding constraints, rejected candidates, and exposure changes. |
| 7. Instrument expression | `instrument_engine` is real: a -5..+5 conviction scale selects equity/credit-spread/debit-spread/LEAPS structures, priced with a real Black-Scholes engine (real spot, real realized volatility, real 10Y rate). No free options-chain source exists, so every options candidate is honestly labeled theoretical-pricing-only and carries a required, unresolved blocker. | Stock, short, call, put, and defined-risk alternatives are compared for an approved target using trustworthy chain history, quotes, implied volatility, skew, Greeks, liquidity, costs, assignment and maximum-loss rules. | Symbol and portfolio candidate views show an expression rationale or a precise unavailable/blocked reason. |
| 8. Shadow operation | There is no repeated production-data decision history or reconciliation process. | Multiple effective dates run manually from sealed inputs through published snapshots; failures, revisions, expected-versus-realized behavior, lifecycle thresholds, and operator review are reconciled without broker orders. | Runs, Strategy Management, Today, and monitoring views establish operational and research readiness separately. |
| 9. Automation | Pipeline locking, normalized input hashes, resumable stages, recovery, alerts, and calendar policy are incomplete. | Idempotency, concurrency, timeout, retry, interruption-recovery, market-calendar, observability, and acceptance tests pass over repeated shadow runs. | Operations may offer scheduling while preserving the same inspectable stage records. |
| 10. Execution boundary | Broker connectivity and order authorization are deliberately absent. | A separately reviewed design covers account reconciliation, approvals, limits, duplicate-order prevention, market-state checks, kill switches, audit, and recovery. Research success alone cannot satisfy this gate. | A future execution surface; nothing in the current desk submits an order. |

## Universe candidates, not recommendations

The production universe will be database-driven and revisioned. Adding a symbol means establishing its stable identity, data coverage, effective eligibility, asset and sleeve classification, liquidity and mandate rules; it never means inserting a ticker into frontend or strategy logic.

[DIA](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-dow-jones-industrial-average-etf-trust-dia) can be evaluated as a US-equity-sleeve candidate, with explicit overlap controls against other broad equity vehicles. For digital-asset research, a governed point-in-time BTC/USD reference series can supply the longer underlying history. [IBIT](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/333011/ishares-bitcoin-trust-etf) is only an execution candidate while it is actually listed, data-ready, liquid and mandate-eligible. BTC history must not be spliced into IBIT history, and a pre-IBIT backtest cannot report an IBIT trade. Their mention here authorizes evaluation only; it is neither evidence, a recommendation, nor a request to add them to the synthetic seed.

## Definition of a real decision

A decision surface becomes real only when it identifies a sealed non-synthetic dataset, effective time, universe revision, strategy or factor revisions, model outputs, constraints, gate status, lineage, freshness, and blockers. Unknown values remain null. A real research decision is still not an authorization to trade.
