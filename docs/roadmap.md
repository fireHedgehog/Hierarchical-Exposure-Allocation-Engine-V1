# Capability sequence from demonstration to real decisions

Edition V1 preserves the durable design; this page defines the implementation sequence and the evidence required before a surface can represent real rather than synthetic decisions. Operations → Pipeline is the live, database-backed readiness view. Each phase should ship as a narrow, visible vertical slice; later gaps do not prevent useful earlier work. Completion means persisted, inspectable behavior in the application, not a persuasive document or one successful backtest.

| Phase | Current gap | Completion evidence | UI surface unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Macro source to state | FRED access can be verified, but no production adapter, point-in-time observations, sealed dataset, or computed regime exists. | Repeatable paced ingestion with ALFRED vintage metadata; timestamp, unit, lineage, freshness, coverage, and revision validation; immutable dataset hash; deterministic manual regime run. | Data Management, Runs, Today, and the regime branch of the hierarchy show real classified inputs and blockers. |
| 2. Security master and universes | The six seeded ETFs are synthetic fixtures. There is no production reference-data adapter or versioned eligibility contract. | Stable exposure, research-reference, security, and provider identities; ticker history, listings, delistings, corporate actions, effective-dated universe membership and reasons; an immutable universe revision tied to a sealed market dataset. | Data Management and Symbols can distinguish underlying research references, the full eligible universe, and the currently available candidate set. |
| 3. Broad risk and sleeve allocation | Regime, net/gross limits, and sleeve weights are demonstration records. | A revisioned policy maps the real state and uncertainty to portfolio risk limits and broad asset or strategy sleeve budgets, with sensitivity tests and explicit null behavior. | Today and Hierarchy explain why capital is or is not available before individual names are considered. |
| 4. Cross-sectional discovery | No real factor engine ranks sectors, industries, or securities. | One revisioned selector with a declared hypothesis and baseline produces point-in-time ranks within peer groups and records coverage, IC, decay, turnover, costs, capacity, stability, and walk-forward evidence. | Cross-section becomes a dynamic discovery surface with ranked candidates and attributable factor contributions. |
| 5. Single-name timing | Symbol signals and chart events are synthetic records; there is no independent entry or exit computation. | A separately revisioned time-series model consumes eligible candidates, emits `enter`, `wait`, `hold`, `exit`, or `none`, and passes leakage, timing, cost, decay, and out-of-sample checks. | Symbol pages explain current timing, chart events, historical decisions, metrics, decay, and failure states. |
| 6. Portfolio construction | No production optimizer reconciles candidate evidence with existing exposures. | Reproducible target weights and deltas satisfy net/gross, covariance, overlap, factor, sector, concentration, liquidity, turnover, and scenario constraints, including honest infeasibility. | Today and Hierarchy show the proposed portfolio, binding constraints, rejected candidates, and exposure changes. |
| 7. Instrument expression | The option example is synthetic; no real chain history, executable quote validation, or expression comparison exists. | Stock, short, call, put, and defined-risk alternatives are compared for an approved target using trustworthy chain history, quotes, implied volatility, skew, Greeks, liquidity, costs, assignment and maximum-loss rules. | Symbol and portfolio candidate views show an expression rationale or a precise unavailable/blocked reason. |
| 8. Shadow operation | There is no repeated production-data decision history or reconciliation process. | Multiple effective dates run manually from sealed inputs through published snapshots; failures, revisions, expected-versus-realized behavior, lifecycle thresholds, and operator review are reconciled without broker orders. | Runs, Strategy Management, Today, and monitoring views establish operational and research readiness separately. |
| 9. Automation | Pipeline locking, normalized input hashes, resumable stages, recovery, alerts, and calendar policy are incomplete. | Idempotency, concurrency, timeout, retry, interruption-recovery, market-calendar, observability, and acceptance tests pass over repeated shadow runs. | Operations may offer scheduling while preserving the same inspectable stage records. |
| 10. Execution boundary | Broker connectivity and order authorization are deliberately absent. | A separately reviewed design covers account reconciliation, approvals, limits, duplicate-order prevention, market-state checks, kill switches, audit, and recovery. Research success alone cannot satisfy this gate. | A future execution surface; nothing in the current desk submits an order. |

## Universe candidates, not recommendations

The production universe will be database-driven and revisioned. Adding a symbol means establishing its stable identity, data coverage, effective eligibility, asset and sleeve classification, liquidity and mandate rules; it never means inserting a ticker into frontend or strategy logic.

[DIA](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-dow-jones-industrial-average-etf-trust-dia) can be evaluated as a US-equity-sleeve candidate, with explicit overlap controls against other broad equity vehicles. For digital-asset research, a governed point-in-time BTC/USD reference series can supply the longer underlying history. [IBIT](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/333011/ishares-bitcoin-trust-etf) is only an execution candidate while it is actually listed, data-ready, liquid and mandate-eligible. BTC history must not be spliced into IBIT history, and a pre-IBIT backtest cannot report an IBIT trade. Their mention here authorizes evaluation only; it is neither evidence, a recommendation, nor a request to add them to the synthetic seed.

## Definition of a real decision

A decision surface becomes real only when it identifies a sealed non-synthetic dataset, effective time, universe revision, strategy or factor revisions, model outputs, constraints, gate status, lineage, freshness, and blockers. Unknown values remain null. A real research decision is still not an authorization to trade.
